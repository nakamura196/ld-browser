Use cases

Published Linked Data opened in Musubi. Click one to open that resource and follow its links from there. One example shows a source that cannot be read.

  • Japan Search

    From a ukiyo-e print to its artist, across national authorities

    The Great Wave off Kanagawa at the Tokyo National Museum. The image is shown, and the artist, period and holder open in place. From Hokusai you can jump to the same person at Wikidata, DBpedia, VIAF and the National Diet Library.

    • Images
    • Nesting
    • Across institutions
    • Graph
    https://jpsearch.go.jp/data/cobas-52087
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • Japan Search

    One person, tied across institutions

    Japan Search's name authority for Katsushika Hokusai. Alternative names are listed with kana and English readings, and owl:sameAs ties him to authorities around the world. The graph shows those ties at a glance.

    • Across institutions
    • Graph
    https://jpsearch.go.jp/entity/chname/葛飾北斎
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • DBpedia

    Linked Data built from Wikipedia

    DBpedia's Hokusai. DBpedia redirects its https URIs to an http address, which a browser page cannot follow; Musubi fetches the Turtle file directly instead.

    • Rewriting
    • Across institutions
    http://dbpedia.org/resource/Hokusai
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • Library of Congress

    A Library of Congress name authority

    Hokusai in the Library of Congress authorities (id.loc.gov). The data comes back as deeply nested JSON-LD; variant names and sources can be read in place.

    • JSON-LD
    • Nesting
    http://id.loc.gov/authorities/names/n80043599
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • GeoNames

    A place name with coordinates

    Tokyo in GeoNames, returned as RDF/XML: names in many languages, coordinates, and a link up to its parent place (Japan).

    • RDF/XML
    https://sws.geonames.org/1850147/
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • Social-experiment case database

    A research project's data, read with its vocabulary

    A case from the University of Tokyo's low-carbon social experiments (sedb). The original domain no longer answers, so Musubi reads the published Turtle instead, along with its own vocabulary for property names.

    • Rewriting
    • Vocabulary
    http://low-carbon.k.u-tokyo.ac.jp/059cc1cca31ac7400e89b9d80ec880cb
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • RailClass

    A rail vehicle class, named from Wikidata

    ICE T in RailClass, a dataset of rail vehicle classes. Operators and lines are named from Wikidata, and its 3D models and livery colours open automatically.

    • Nesting
    • Images
    • Rewriting
    https://w3id.org/railclass/Q696668
    Open in MusubiSource site
  • W3C

    Look up a vocabulary term

    SKOS's skos:prefLabel. Open a property URI as it is and read its definition. A redirect step loses the CORS permission here, so Musubi fetches the end of the redirect directly.

    • Vocabulary
    • RDF/XML
    • Rewriting
    http://www.w3.org/2004/02/skos/core#prefLabel
    Open in Musubi
  • schema.org

    Pull data out of a web page

    The schema.org page for about. It is an ordinary HTML page; Musubi extracts the JSON-LD, microdata and RDFa embedded in it.

    • Embedded in HTML
    • Vocabulary
    https://schema.org/about
    Open in Musubi
  • National Diet Library

    Sources without CORS, read through the relay

    Hokusai in the National Diet Library authorities. It returns RDF but does not allow cross-origin reads (CORS), so a browser alone cannot load it. For such sources only, Musubi fetches through a relay server.

    • Relay
    • RDF/XML
    http://id.ndl.go.jp/auth/entity/00270331
    Open in MusubiSource site

Musubi
